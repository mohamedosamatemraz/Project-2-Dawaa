🔄 Workflows

Two n8n workflows, four flows.

💬 1. Customer request

Trigger: WhatsApp message

  1. Normalize the message, check if it is a photo
  2. Download the photo or read the text
  3. AI reads the request, then match it with the catalog
  4. Route: nothing found, or one row per medicine
  5. Save the reservation lines
  6. Auto-reserve and take stock, or ask the pharmacist
  7. Confirm to the customer on WhatsApp

🧑‍⚕️ 2. Pharmacist decision

Trigger: Telegram button (callback)

  1. Parse the decision and find the pending lines
  2. Read the catalog and apply the decision
  3. Answer the pharmacist and edit the Telegram message
  4. Update the lines
  5. Approved: take the stock and tell the customer
  6. Rejected: tell the customer

🚨 3. Error handling

Trigger: Error Trigger

  1. Classify the error
  2. Append a row to the error sheet
  3. Alert the team on Telegram
  4. If needed, send an email

⏰ 4. Scheduled jobs

Trigger: every 15 minutes and every day at 22:00

  1. 15 min: find expired reservations, mark them, notify the client and update the stock
  2. 22:00: find low stock and post the reorder list on Telegram and by email
